## Warnings & Uncertainties

1. **DOL fact sheet URLs are unstable.** 7 of 22 URLs returned 404. DOL appears to reorganize fact sheet paths periodically. The `/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/` path structure may have changed.
2. **DOL returns 403 for some pages** when accessed programmatically (Fact Sheet #15). These pages likely exist but require a browser or different request pattern.
3. **Content assessor has blind spots** for non-wage-amount laws. Equal pay, tip pooling rules, and reimbursement laws don't contain dollar amounts, so the assessor marks them "unsure" or "not actionable" even when the page is correct. This is a known limitation of the minimum_wage heuristic being applied to non-wage categories.
4. **Premium Pay and Reimbursements** have limited federal coverage for private sector. These categories are primarily governed by state law. The federal inventory for these categories is inherently thin.
5. **No auto-promotion occurred.** All candidates remain in quarantine for manual review.
